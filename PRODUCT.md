# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Anyone sharing a home, equally flatmates and families. Flatmates in their twenties in a rented flat or PG
open it on the go to log a grocery run, see who owes whom, or tick off a chore. Families use it to run
shared bills, groceries and chores across mixed ages. Most sessions are short and one-handed: a phone in
the kitchen by day, in bed at night.

## Product Purpose

Flockr is the single source of truth for a shared home: expenses and exact balances, recurring bills,
metered usage, chores, shopping, chat and documents. Success is that nobody argues about money, because
every number is right to the paisa and every screen answers "what do I owe, what's next" at a glance.

## Brand Personality

Warm, playful, trustworthy. Friendly and a little cheeky, like a good flatmate, while the money stays
serious and exact. Copy is human and short. References: Things and Todoist for calm polish and sharp
details, CRED and Jupiter for tactile confidence, Monzo and Revolut for clear balances and friendly colour.

## Anti-references

- Stock Material template: default purple and slate tones, a card inside every card, Google sample-app look.
- Corporate fintech: navy and gold, dense tables, banking-portal feel.
- Crypto neon: glowing gradients and glass on black.
- Childish or cartoonish: heavy illustrations and mascots. It handles real money.

## Design Principles

1. The balance is the headline. Every money screen leads with what you owe or are owed, in words and a sign, never colour alone.
2. Warmth in the details, precision in the numbers. Personality lives in copy, colour and motion moments; figures stay plain and exact.
3. One glance, one thumb. The next action on any screen is reachable one-handed and obvious without reading.
4. Earned familiarity. Standard Android patterns, restyled so nothing looks stock.
5. Both themes are first-class. Light and dark get equal care.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast for text and controls in both themes. Honour the system reduced-motion setting. Money
direction is carried by words and signs as well as colour.
